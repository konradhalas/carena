Carena

Buggies with cannons in a dirt arena. Drift, jump and shoot your friends.

What it is

One big gun

Every hit knocks the other car sideways. Four hits and it's scrap — make your twelve shells count.

Stunt-show driving

Handbrake drifts, big jumps, a bridge to race over or under. Rolled over? Keep driving on your roof.

Play with friends

Host a game in one click and let your friends join, or hop onto a server.

Quick, messy matches

Most wrecks when the clock runs out wins. Grab repair kits and ammo to stay in the fight.

Play

  1. Download the game for Windows, macOS or Linux, unzip it and start it.
    On a Mac, if it won't open: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  2. Pick a name, type in the server address and hit Join game.
  3. Or click Host a game and give your friends your IP address.
